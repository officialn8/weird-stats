Do your bath wrinkles make a new pattern every time?

You’ve seen your fingers prune. Have you ever looked closely at the lines?

Reveal the discovery

Some of the same lines return.

Researchers photographed the same fingertips after two soaks at least a day apart. The skin had smoothed out between soaks, but major wrinkles returned in similar places and directions.

This preliminary 2025 study involved three adults. Some wrinkles differed between soaks.

The study behind the wrinkles

Three adults, ages 19–22, took part. Researchers photographed all five fingers on each person’s right hand after two 30-minute soaks in 40°C water, at least 24 hours apart. They matched visible wrinkles and compared their directions.

Some lines did not match. Matching wrinkles by hand leaves room for bias. The study did not establish a permanent, unique wrinkle pattern or prove the proposed blood-vessel explanation. Measuring several wrinkles on each hand does not increase the number of participants.
