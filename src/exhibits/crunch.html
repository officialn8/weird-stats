Your ears help decide how fresh a chip seems.

Researchers changed the biting sounds people heard through headphones. With louder or enhanced high-frequency feedback, the chips seemed crisper and fresher.

In informal questions after this small 2004 experiment, 15 of 20 participants thought the chips came from different packages. They came from the same package. This is not a population estimate or a test of actual freshness.
