Source & how to read this

The Rijksmuseum’s 2022 image represents a 0.005 mm square of paint per pixel. A 1 mm square therefore contains 200 × 200 pixels; a 0.1 mm square contains 20 × 20. The magnified grids are measured diagrams, not microscopic photographs or actual paint colors.

Which holds more copper: a modern U.S. penny or a nickel?

The nickel contains about60×as much copper.
Penny0.0625 g copper
Nickel3.75 g copper

Each square is one penny’s copper content.

Pick a coin, or skip the guess.

A penny is copper-coated zinc. A nickel is mostly copper and weighs twice as much.

Source & how to read this

U.S. Mint coin specifications ↗: penny, 2.5 g × 2.5% copper = 0.0625 g; nickel, 5 g × 75% copper = 3.75 g. Dividing gives 60.

These amounts come from rounded manufacturing specifications, not measurements of individual coins. Equal squares represent equal copper mass. Coin illustrations do not depict composition by area.
