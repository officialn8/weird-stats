## Audio

- Recording: [Eating potato chips 1](https://bigsoundbank.com/eating-potato-chips-1-s1430.html), Joseph Sardin, BigSoundBank #1430.
- License: CC0, as stated on the source page checked October 4, 2026.
- Download: `https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/1430.mp3`.
- Both files use the same first 1.65 seconds of the source recording, with a 110 Hz high-pass filter, 15 ms fade-in, and 150 ms fade-out starting at 1.5 seconds.
- A: 1,400 Hz low-pass filter. B: 7 dB treble gain at 3,000 Hz.
- Each processed with FFmpeg `loudnorm=I=-21:TP=-3:LRA=5`, then encoded mono, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps MP3. This is not a calibrated loudness match or a reproduction of the study protocol.

These are separate illustrative recordings, not participant audio. No microphone or headphones permission is requested. Audio is loaded only after a Listen click. The page never autoplays it. The illustration's waveform follows the browser analyser output, not a simulated study measurement.
