## Same two seats

Record: `content/entries/same-two-seats.json`.
Local preview: http://localhost:63214/#same-two-seats.

The draft compares California and Wyoming's fixed 2020 Census resident populations with their equal allocated U.S. Senate seats. The primary evidence is [Census Table E](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/data/apportionment/apportionment-2020-tableE.pdf) and the [Senate's account of equal state representation](https://www.senate.gov/about/origins-foundations/senate-and-constitution.htm). Both were opened and read for this packet. The population ratio is calculated from the two counts and rounded to one decimal place.

The treatment toggles between residents and seats. Both start at zero, with units and maxima visible. The table and CSV contain both views. The qualification distinguishes resident counts from voters, a historical snapshot from current estimates, and seat allocation from individual voting power.
