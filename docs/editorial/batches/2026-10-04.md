## California versus 21 states

Local preview: http://localhost:63214/#same-two-seats.

The original California/Wyoming framing was replaced after review flagged its familiarity, question/answer mismatch, and uninformative equal-seat bars. The new question asks how many states combined have fewer residents than California. The reveal answers: the 21 least-populous states, combined, with 42 Senate seats to California’s two. Their combined 2020 population is 37,174,921, below California’s 39,538,223. Those groups are allocated 42 and 2 Senate seats respectively.

Both primary sources were reopened: [Census Table E](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/data/apportionment/apportionment-2020-tableE.pdf) for the resident counts and ranks, and the [U.S. Senate](https://www.senate.gov/about/origins-foundations/senate-and-constitution.htm) for two senators per state. The historical period is April 1, 2020, not a current estimate. Source-check date: October 4, 2026, Central time.
